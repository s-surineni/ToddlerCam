# Privacy Policy for GiggleCam

**Last updated: June 22, 2026**

## Data Collection

GiggleCam does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on-device.

## Camera Access

GiggleCam requires camera access solely to display the live camera preview and take photos. No images or video are sent off the device.

## Photo Storage

If you opt in via the settings menu, photos you take may be saved to your device's local gallery. This is entirely optional and controlled by you. No photos are uploaded or shared.

## Third-Party Services

GiggleCam does **not** use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

## Children's Privacy

GiggleCam is designed for use by toddlers and young children. We do not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be reflected here. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the email associated with your Google Play Developer account.
