# Privacy Policy for GiggleCam

**Last updated: June 25, 2026**

This privacy policy describes how GiggleCam ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application ("App").

## Information We Do NOT Collect

GiggleCam is designed with children's privacy as a top priority. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- **No account creation** – The App does not require any sign-up or registration.
- **No analytics** – We do not use any analytics SDKs or tracking tools.
- **No advertising** – The App contains no ads or ad-serving frameworks.
- **No data collection** – We do not collect any usage data, device information, or crash logs.
- **No internet access** – The App operates fully offline and does not connect to any servers.

## Camera Usage

The App uses your device's camera to display a live preview with fun animal overlays. Camera data is processed **entirely on-device** and is never transmitted, stored, or shared.

## Photo Storage

If you choose to save a photo, it is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to these photos.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

GiggleCam is designed for children of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information from users.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
